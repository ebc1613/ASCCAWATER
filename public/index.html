Reads about 1 ft lower than the tank's physical gauge.

The pressure sensor taps into the tank about 1 foot above the floor, so it only measures the water sitting above the sensor. The physical gauge on the tank measures all the way down to the floor. Because of that 1-foot head start, the level shown here will always read roughly 1 foot less than the physical gauge — that difference is expected and correct, not a fault.
